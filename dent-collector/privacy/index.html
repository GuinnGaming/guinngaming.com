Privacy Policy for Dent Collector

Effective and last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming ("we", "us") handles information when you play Dent Collector (the "Game") on Android. The Game is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information we collect

Saved on your device. The Game saves progress, ships, paints, settings, Stardust and other in-game currencies or items locally. GuinnGaming does not receive a copy of local game progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local data, not records already held by Google.

Not requested through the Game. There is no game account sign-up, chat or user-generated content. The Game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or photos. If you email us, we receive the email and information included in it.

Service data. Google's advertising services process information described below, including identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. No precise location is collected. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address even though the Game has no Android location permission.

The Game does not enable Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics or Unity Analytics, and it does not initialize Unity IAP or another purchasing service.

Android permissions

These are plain-language explanations of permission capabilities, not claims that each capability is continuously used:

  • INTERNET: lets the Game and its services communicate online, including ads.
  • VIBRATE: allows vibration feedback.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets services check network availability.
  • AD_ID and Android AdServices identifier permissions: allow Google's advertising service to use the advertising ID and related advertising identifiers.
  • WAKE_LOCK: allows the app or an SDK to keep the device awake while completing an operation.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: allow an app or SDK operation to run as an Android foreground service, including one that transfers data; these permissions do not themselves mean the Game runs continuously in the background.
  • BILLING: the current Android build includes the Play Billing permission and related library code. This version of the Game does not use that permission to offer purchases, process payments, or send purchase or payment information.

An additional app-specific permission, com.guinngaming.spaceshooter.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, restricts access to internal broadcast receivers.

The Game has no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), including optional rewarded ads. Every ad request is non-personalized. The Game requests and uses the advertising ID for advertising. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is collected. Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of G, and no ad mediation partners are used. The Game checks regional privacy choices using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before initializing advertising. Where a consent form is required, UMP presents it. You can revisit required privacy options in Settings → Privacy & Ads. Your choice never enables personalized ads. If a consent check fails, ads are allowed only when UMP confirms a valid prior choice permits them.

Google's ad service processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID, other device or session identifiers, ad interactions such as taps/views, and diagnostics. These data support service operation, ad delivery/measurement, and fraud prevention/security as applicable. Ads are not personalized to your interests.

The Game does not show interstitial, pop-up or app-open ads. Ads appear only as a banner on selected menu screens (never during a level) once you have progressed far enough, and as optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus such as extra Stardust or to save a win streak.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

3. In-app purchases

The Game does not currently offer in-app purchases. A store screen may show upcoming items marked as coming soon. No payment is processed in this version, and the Game does not receive credit-card or bank details.

As noted in section 1, the current Android build still includes a Play Billing permission and related library code. That code is not initialized, and this version does not send purchase history or payment information to Google or Unity.

4. How we use information

Local information runs the Game and remembers progress/settings. The advertising provider processes information in section 2 for its applicable service functions, measurement/analytics, diagnostics and security. We use support messages to answer questions and handle privacy requests.

GuinnGaming does not run a game-account server. We do not sell personal information. Non-personalized ads do not eliminate all service data collection.

5. Sharing

The Game sends information to Google through AdMob, as described above. Google processes that information under its service terms and privacy disclosures. Google ad data is disclosed as shared in our Google Play Data safety section.

Support emails are also processed by the email services used to receive and answer them. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local progress stays on the device until app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. GuinnGaming holds no copy of that progress.

Google controls retention of advertising records under its policies.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete Google records. You can contact us at GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of information we hold or that our providers process. We will address information we hold and help route requests concerning provider-held data through the applicable provider process, subject to lawful retention requirements.

7. Children

The Game is for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. If a parent or guardian contacts us about personal information collected from a child under 13, we will delete it. Contact GuinnGaming87@gmail.com. We will address information we hold and coordinate deletion of provider-held information through the applicable provider process.

8. Security

GuinnGaming does not run a game-data backend or store your payment-card details. Providers process data as described above. The application disallows cleartext traffic, and Google discloses encryption in transit. No electronic system is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Email GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your information, or to exercise other applicable rights. We may need to verify your request. We will address information we hold and help route requests concerning provider-held data through the applicable provider process, subject to lawful retention requirements.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or privacy requirements change. We will post the updated version at https://guinngaming.com/dent-collector/privacy/ and update its dates. We will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
