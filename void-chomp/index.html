Void Chomp

Hole Lotta Snacks! Eat it all, grow bigger.

You're the Void, a hungry little monster with a bottomless appetite. Chomp crumbs, candy and cookies, grow bigger with every bite, and work your way up to donuts, toys and books. Clear every goal before the timer runs out, then unlock new monsters and skins.

Coming to Android onGoogle Play

Free to play · Android · Ages 13+

A vacuum with teeth

Get close and your Void pulls food in. Round treats roll toward your mouth, flat ones slide and tip over the lip, and big items get a satisfying chomp, shake and burp.

  • Follow the chain: food is laid out in snaking curves, zigzags, spirals and rings. Ride one chain straight into the next.
  • Grow into it: too big to swallow yet? Shove it around until the goal bar says it fits.
  • Watch out for bombs: they come in three sizes and cost you time and size. Steer around them, or grab a Shield.
  • One finger: simple drag controls with adjustable move speed.

Six worlds to devour

Every world is packed with its own things to swallow, from tiny snacks to big stuff you'll need to grow into.

Pick your monster

Start as Chomper, then unlock Goofball and Xeno, each with its own lips, teeth and glowing eyes, and paint them in a rainbow of colors. Beat level 10 and the Kraken bursts out of the treasure chest.

Unlock them with coins you earn by playing.

Power up

Pick up to 3 power-ups for every run.

  • Snack Snag: pull food in from farther away.
  • Belly Boost: an instant size boost.
  • Snack Time: extra seconds on the clock.
  • Shield: bombs bounce off for 10 seconds.

Fair and free to play

Every level can be beaten without paying. Lives refill on their own over time, one every 5 minutes, and winning a level never costs a life.

Earn coins as you play, then spend them on new monsters, colors and power-ups.
