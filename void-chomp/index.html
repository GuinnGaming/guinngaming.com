Void Chomp

Hole Lotta Snacks

You're the Void, a hungry little monster with a bottomless appetite. Chomp crumbs, candy and cookies, grow bigger with every bite, and work your way up to donuts, toys and books. Clear every goal before the timer runs out, then unlock new monsters and skins.

How it plays

A vacuum
with teeth.

Get close and your Void pulls food in. Round treats roll toward your mouth, flat ones slide and tip over the lip, and big items get a satisfying chomp, shake and burp.

  1. Follow the chainFood is laid out in snaking curves, zigzags, spirals and rings. Ride one chain straight into the next.
  2. Grow into itToo big to swallow yet? Shove it around until the goal bar says it fits.
  3. Watch out for bombsThey come in three sizes and cost you time and size. Steer around them, or grab a Shield.
  4. One fingerSimple drag controls with adjustable move speed.

Pick your monster

Unlock the Kraken.

Start as Chomper, then unlock Goofball and Xeno, each with its own lips, teeth and glowing eyes, and paint them in a rainbow of colors. Beat level 10 and the Kraken bursts out of the treasure chest.

FAIR
PLAY

Fair and free to play.

Every level can be beaten without paying. Lives refill on their own over time, one every 5 minutes, and winning a level never costs a life. Earn coins as you play, then spend them on new monsters, colors and power-ups.
