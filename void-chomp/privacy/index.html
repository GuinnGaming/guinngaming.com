Privacy Policy for Void Chomp: Hole Lotta Snacks

Effective and last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming ("we", "us") handles information when you play Void Chomp: Hole Lotta Snacks ("Void Chomp", the "Game") on Android. The Game is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information we collect

Saved on your device. The Game saves progress, coins, lives, power-ups, monster and skin choices, settings and purchase unlocks locally. GuinnGaming does not receive a copy of local game progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local data, not records already held by Google or Unity.

Not requested through the Game. There is no game account sign-up, chat or user-generated content. The Game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or photos. If you email us, we receive the email and information included in it.

Service data. Google advertising/billing services and Unity's purchasing service process information described below, including identifiers, purchase information and diagnostics. No precise location is collected. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address even though the Game has no Android location permission.

The Game does not enable Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics or Unity Analytics. This does not mean there is no telemetry: Unity IAP sends required purchasing data described in section 3.

Android permissions

These are plain-language explanations of permission capabilities, not claims that each capability is continuously used:

  • INTERNET: lets the Game and its services communicate online, including ads and purchases.
  • VIBRATE: allows vibration feedback.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets services check network availability.
  • BILLING: allows purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • WAKE_LOCK: allows the app or an SDK to keep the device awake while completing an operation.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: allows an app or SDK operation to run as an Android foreground service; the permission does not itself mean the Game runs continuously in the background.

An additional app-specific permission, com.guinngaming.voidchomp.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, restricts access to internal broadcast receivers.

The Game has no advertising-ID (AD_ID) or Android AdServices identifier permissions, and no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), including optional rewarded ads. Every ad request is non-personalized. The Game does not request or collect an advertising ID. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is collected. Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of G, and no ad mediation partners are used. The Game checks regional privacy choices using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before initializing advertising. Where a consent form is required, UMP presents it. You can revisit required privacy options in Settings → Privacy & Ads. Your choice never enables personalized ads. If a consent check fails, ads are allowed only when UMP confirms a valid prior choice permits them.

Google's ad service processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers, ad interactions such as taps/views, and diagnostics. Removing the advertising-ID permission does not remove app-set, install or session identifiers. These data support service operation, ad delivery/measurement, and fraud prevention/security as applicable. Ads are not personalized to your interests.

The Game does not show interstitial, pop-up or app-open ads. Ads appear only as a banner on the main screen and as optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus. Remove Ads (or an active Monster Pass) hides the banner; rewarded ads stay optional.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

3. In-app purchases

Optional purchases use Google Play Billing, integrated through Unity IAP 5.4.4. Products include monsters, coin packs, bundles, skins, Remove Ads and Monster Pass (30 days). Monster Pass is non-renewing, not an automatically renewing subscription.

Google handles payment. The Game does not receive your credit-card or bank details. Purchase information is needed to process and verify purchases, deliver purchases, prevent duplicate delivery and restore eligible purchases. You can use Google Play purchase authentication to manage purchases.

Use the Game's restore option with the Google Play account that made the purchase to restore eligible non-consumable purchases, such as permanent unlocks. Consumables are not restored this way. Monster Pass time is local and is not restorable after reinstalling.

Unity IAP data needed for purchasing. Unity IAP sends required purchase/transaction events and related data to Unity, the purchasing-service provider. This processing is necessary to operate the Game’s purchasing functions, including processing and verifying purchases. Unity's disclosure for this service includes purchase history, SDK/personal identifiers, device or other identifiers, crash logs and diagnostics/other app-performance information. Unity's disclosure also describes country/IP processing for approximate location. These are service identifiers, not names you are asked to enter in the Game.

Unity's purchasing data supports app functionality and analytics associated with the purchasing service. Required data is sent even though the Game sets Unity advertising and analytics consent intents to Denied, and even though no Unity Analytics package is enabled. Denial does not stop required purchase events and identifiers. Advertising identifiers are suppressed, but other identifiers and required purchasing data are not.

See Unity IAP privacy and consent and Unity IAP's Google Play data disclosure.

4. How we use information

Local information runs the Game and remembers progress/settings. Purchase information supports fulfillment, eligible restoration and duplicate-delivery prevention. The providers process information in sections 2–3 for their applicable service functions, measurement/analytics, diagnostics and security. We use support messages to answer questions and handle privacy requests.

GuinnGaming does not run a game-account server. We do not sell personal information. Non-personalized ads and denied consent intents do not eliminate all service data collection.

5. Sharing

The Game sends information to Google through AdMob and Google Play Billing, and to Unity through Unity IAP, as described above. Providers process information under their service terms and privacy disclosures. Google ad data is disclosed as shared in our Google Play Data safety section. Unity acts as a service provider for purchasing; data still leaves your device and goes to Unity.

Support emails are also processed by the email services used to receive and answer them. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local progress stays on the device until app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. GuinnGaming holds no copy of that progress. Eligible non-consumables can be restored through Google Play; consumable and Monster Pass local history is not protected by restoration.

Google controls retention of advertising and Play purchase records under its policies. Unity's IAP disclosure describes service logs retained for 30 days and transaction records retained for service/reporting.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete Google or Unity records. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information we hold or that our providers process. Unity IAP has no self-service deletion API; developer-directed processor requests go through Unity IAP support.

7. Children

The Game is for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. If a parent or guardian contacts us about personal information collected from a child under 13, we will delete it. Contact GuinnGaming87@gmail.com. We will address information we hold and coordinate deletion of provider-held information through the applicable provider process.

8. Security

GuinnGaming does not run a game-data backend or store your payment-card details. Providers process data as described above. The application disallows cleartext traffic, and Google and Unity disclose encryption in transit. No electronic system is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Email GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your information, or to exercise other applicable rights. We may need to verify your request. We will address information we hold and help route requests concerning provider-held data through the applicable provider process, subject to lawful retention requirements.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or privacy requirements change. We will post the updated version at https://guinngaming.com/void-chomp/privacy/ and update its dates. We will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
