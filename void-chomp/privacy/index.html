Privacy Policy for Void Chomp: Hole Lotta Snacks

Effective and last updated: November 22, 2026 (adds an age screen and personalized ads for consenting adults)

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming ("we", "us") handles information when you play Void Chomp: Hole Lotta Snacks ("Void Chomp", the "Game") on Android. The Game is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13. It uses a neutral age screen so that players under 16, and players whose age is not known, get child-safe ads only, while adults who agree can get personalized ads.

1. Information we collect

Saved on your device. The Game saves progress, coins, lives, power-ups, monster and skin choices, settings, purchase unlocks and your age-screen answer (see section 2) locally. GuinnGaming does not receive a copy of local game progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local data, not records already held by Google or Unity.

Not requested through the Game. There is no game account sign-up, chat or user-generated content. The Game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or photos. If you email us, we receive the email and information included in it.

Service data. Google advertising/billing services and Unity's purchasing service process information described below, including identifiers, purchase information and diagnostics. No precise location is collected. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address even though the Game has no Android location permission.

The Game does not enable Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics or Unity Analytics. This does not mean there is no telemetry: the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends the advertising data described in section 3, and Unity IAP sends required purchasing data described in section 4.

Android permissions

These are plain-language explanations of permission capabilities, not claims that each capability is continuously used:

  • INTERNET: lets the Game and its services communicate online, including ads and purchases.
  • VIBRATE: allows vibration feedback.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets services check network availability.
  • BILLING: allows purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • WAKE_LOCK: allows the app or an SDK to keep the device awake while completing an operation.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: allows an app or SDK operation to run as an Android foreground service; the permission does not itself mean the Game runs continuously in the background.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the Android AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. They allow ad identifiers and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs to be used only on the adult path (section 3). For children and players whose age is not known, ad requests are tagged so that the advertising ID is not sent.

An additional app-specific permission, com.guinngaming.voidchomp.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, restricts access to internal broadcast receivers.

The Game has no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

2. Age screen

The first time you open the Game, it asks for your year of birth on a neutral age screen. It does not encourage any particular answer and has no answer filled in. The Game uses the year only to choose an age bracket, child or adult, and saves only that bracket on your device. The year of birth itself is not stored and is not sent to us or to Google.

A player counts as an adult only if the year shows they are at least 16, counted cautiously (as if their birthday has not happened yet this year). Everyone else is treated as a child. If you tap Not now, nothing is saved, you are treated as a child, and the screen is shown again the next time the Game starts. To change a saved answer, clear the Game's app data in Android settings. Note that this also clears saved progress.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) for a banner on the main screen and for optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus. The Game does not show interstitial, pop-up or app-open ads, and no ad mediation partners are used. Remove Ads (or an active Monster Pass) hides the banner; rewarded ads stay optional.

Children and players of unknown age. Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment (Google's age-restricted "child" setting, which covers COPPA child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment). Ads are non-personalized and limited to a maximum content rating of G. With this setting, Google disables interest-based and remarketing ads and the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not send the Android advertising ID. Google's consent form is not shown on this path, and you cannot turn personalized ads on.

Adults. Before ads load, the Game checks privacy choices with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice. In US states with privacy laws that apply, Google's US state privacy message lets you opt out. If you agree, ads may be personalized, based on your choices and only where your device settings allow it (for example, Android lets you delete your advertising ID). If you do not agree, or where personalized ads are not allowed, ads are non-personalized. When your region requires it, a Privacy & Ads entry in the Game's Settings lets you review or change your choices at any time. Ads are not loaded until UMP confirms they may be requested.

Google's ad service processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers (such as app-set or session identifiers, and the advertising ID on the adult path where allowed), ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics. These data support ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and security. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is processed.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

4. In-app purchases

Optional purchases use Google Play Billing, integrated through Unity IAP 5.4.4. Products include monsters, coin packs, bundles, skins, Remove Ads and Monster Pass (30 days). Monster Pass is non-renewing, not an automatically renewing subscription.

Google handles payment. The Game does not receive your credit-card or bank details. Purchase information is needed to process and verify purchases, deliver purchases, prevent duplicate delivery and restore eligible purchases. You can use Google Play purchase authentication to manage purchases.

Use the Game's restore option with the Google Play account that made the purchase to restore eligible non-consumable purchases, such as permanent unlocks. Consumables are not restored this way. Monster Pass time is local and is not restorable after reinstalling.

Unity IAP data needed for purchasing. Unity IAP sends required purchase/transaction events and related data to Unity, the purchasing-service provider. This processing is necessary to operate the Game’s purchasing functions, including processing and verifying purchases. Unity's disclosure for this service includes purchase history, SDK/personal identifiers, device or other identifiers, crash logs and diagnostics/other app-performance information. Unity's disclosure also describes country/IP processing for approximate location. These are service identifiers, not names you are asked to enter in the Game.

Unity's purchasing data supports app functionality and analytics associated with the purchasing service. Required data is sent even though the Game sets Unity advertising and analytics consent intents to Denied, and even though no Unity Analytics package is enabled. Denial does not stop required purchase events and identifiers. Advertising identifiers are suppressed, but other identifiers and required purchasing data are not.

See Unity IAP privacy and consent and Unity IAP's Google Play data disclosure.

5. How we use information

Local information runs the Game and remembers progress, settings and your age bracket. Purchase information supports fulfillment, eligible restoration and duplicate-delivery prevention. The providers process information in sections 3–4 for their applicable service functions, measurement/analytics, diagnostics and security. We use support messages to answer questions and handle privacy requests.

GuinnGaming does not run a game-account server. We do not sell personal information. Personalized ads are used only on the adult path after the required consent or choice. Non-personalized ads still involve some service data collection, as described in section 3.

6. Sharing

The Game sends information to Google through AdMob and Google Play Billing, and to Unity through Unity IAP, as described above. Providers process information under their service terms and privacy disclosures. Google ad data is disclosed as shared in our Google Play Data safety section. Unity acts as a service provider for purchasing; data still leaves your device and goes to Unity.

Support emails are also processed by the email services used to receive and answer them. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local progress and your age bracket stay on the device until app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. GuinnGaming holds no copy of that progress. Eligible non-consumables can be restored through Google Play; consumable and Monster Pass local history is not protected by restoration.

Google controls retention of advertising and Play purchase records under its policies. Unity's IAP disclosure describes service logs retained for 30 days and transaction records retained for service/reporting.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete Google or Unity records. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information we hold or that our providers process. Unity IAP has no self-service deletion API; developer-directed processor requests go through Unity IAP support.

8. Children

The Game is for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. Players under 16, and players who skip the age-screen question, receive child-directed, G-rated, non-personalized ads, and the advertising ID is not used on that path, as described in sections 2 and 3. If a parent or guardian contacts us about personal information collected from a child under 13, we will delete it. Contact GuinnGaming87@gmail.com. We will address information we hold and coordinate deletion of provider-held information through the applicable provider process.

9. Security

GuinnGaming does not run a game-data backend or store your payment-card details. Providers process data as described above. The application disallows cleartext traffic, and Google and Unity disclose encryption in transit. No electronic system is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Adults can change their ad choices at any time through Settings → Privacy & Ads, where that option is offered. Email GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your information, or to exercise other applicable rights. We may need to verify your request. We will address information we hold and help route requests concerning provider-held data through the applicable provider process, subject to lawful retention requirements.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or privacy requirements change. We will post the updated version at https://guinngaming.com/void-chomp/privacy/ and update its dates. We will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
