Privacy Policy for Fill The Void

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming (“we”, “us”) handles information when you play Fill The Void (the “Game”) on Android.

1. Information we collect

Information stored on your device. The Game saves your progress, coins, lives, power-ups, monster and color choices, and settings locally on your device. We do not receive this data, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone, and the Game has no account sign-up.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to get extra lives, coins or power-ups). Ads are provided only by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). We do not use any other ad networks. If we add one, we will update this policy before it is used.

Our ad partner may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, completed views)
  • crash and performance diagnostics related to ads

This information is used to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalized. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in the Game's Settings (the gear button in the hub) under “Privacy & Ads”.

3. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs, skins, power-ups, lives, Remove Ads and the Monster Pass. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. Google provides us with limited purchase information (such as a purchase token, product ID and order status) so we can deliver what you bought and restore purchases. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by Google's privacy policy and the Google Play terms.

You can stop in-app purchases from your device with Google Play's purchase authentication settings (Google Play > Settings > Authentication).

4. How we use information

We use information only to run the Game, deliver purchases, show and measure ads, keep the Game fair (for example, the lives timer), and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob and Google Play), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our players and the Game.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for ads and purchases is retained under Google's policies. To ask about any data related to you, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

The Game is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

The Google Play Families Policy and COPPA child-directed requirements do not apply, because the Game is not designed for or targeted at children.

8. Security

We rely on Google's secure systems for ads and payments and do not store personal information on our own servers.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version at https://guinngaming.com/fill-the-void/privacy/ and update the date above.
