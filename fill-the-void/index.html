Fill The Void

Swallow everything. Grow huge.

You're the Void, a hungry monster mouth. Start tiny, swallow crumbs and candy, then grow big enough to gulp down donuts, toys, books and more. Clear every goal before the timer runs out!

Coming soon toGoogle Play

Free to play · Android · Ages 13+

A vacuum with teeth

Get close and your Void pulls food in. Round treats roll toward your mouth, flat ones slide and tip over the lip, and big items get a satisfying chomp, shake and burp.

  • Follow the chain: food is laid out in snaking curves, zigzags, spirals and rings. Ride one chain straight into the next.
  • Grow into it: too big to swallow yet? Shove it around until the goal bar says it fits.
  • Watch out for bombs: they come in three sizes and cost you time and size. Steer around them, or grab a Shield.
  • One finger: simple drag controls with adjustable move speed.

Six worlds to devour

Every world is packed with its own things to swallow, from tiny snacks to big stuff you'll need to grow into.

Pick your monster

Unlock Chomper, Goofball, Jaws and Xeno, each with its own lips, teeth and glowing eyes. Then paint them in a rainbow of colors and special skins.

Collect them with coins you earn by playing, or grab them in the Store.

Power up

Pick up to 3 power-ups for every run.

Giant bosses and events

Giant bosses wait along the way. Dodge their telegraphed slams, keep chipping away and win big coin rewards.

Check the Events tab for limited-time holiday events with their own levels, a special boss and exclusive skins you can only win while the event is on. Past events include Monster Mash, a Halloween event with three spooky stages and the Pumpkin King, and more holiday events are on the way.

Fair and free to play

Every level can be beaten without paying. Lives refill on their own over time, and you can earn free coins, power-ups and extra lives from the Free Rewards screen.

The optional Store has coin packs, skins, power-up bundles, a Remove Ads option and the Monster Pass for players who want a boost. You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads in Settings › Privacy & Ads.
