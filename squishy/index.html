Squishy

Full of Snacks!

Meet Squishy, a bouncy little belly with a big appetite and a whole lot of snacks inside. Drag and poke the belly with your finger to slosh everything around. Line up three or more of the same treat and they burst in a shower of color and crumbs. Chain matches together for bigger, brighter combos!

How it plays

Drag, match,
pop.

Snacks tumble around in the belly, and it's your job to squish them into matches.

  1. Poke and sloshDrag and poke the belly with your finger to move everything around.
  2. Match three or moreLine up the same treat and they burst in a shower of color and crumbs.
  3. Chain itString matches together for bigger, brighter combos.
Pick a power

Six powers,
pick three.

Spend crumbs you earn on powers in the shop, keep them in your inventory, and pick three different ones before a level. Tap a power and it fires instantly.

  • Flavor PodClears every snack of one kind.
  • Bubble PodSinks, swells, and pops to shake up the pile.
  • Jelly BellRolls the belly walls in waves so every snack finds a new spot.
  • Big GulpGulps 6 to 8 snacks from the middle of the belly.
  • Milkshake SlurpPulls snacks in toward the center.
  • Second HelpingStarts your next match at x2 and doubles the crumbs you earn.

Watch out for bombs in later levels. They count down with your moves, then blow up nearby snacks. Those snacks still count toward your goals, and bombs never hurt Squishy or take a life.

Play your way

Relax, score
or travel.

  1. RelaxAn endless board you can come back to any time.
  2. ScoreChase your best total.
  3. Travel the level pathClear the goals, and unlock new looks with the crumbs you earn.

Dress Squishy up, pick a background, and choose a name from a menu of tasty words. No typing needed, and the name stays on your device.
