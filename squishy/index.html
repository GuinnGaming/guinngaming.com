Squishy

Full of Snacks! Squish snacks in a wobbly belly.

Meet Squishy, a bouncy little belly with a big appetite and a whole lot of snacks inside. Drag and poke the belly with your finger to slosh everything around. Line up three or more of the same treat and they burst in a shower of color and crumbs. Chain matches together for bigger, brighter combos!

Free to play · Android · Rated Everyone

Drag, match, pop

Snacks tumble around in the belly, and it's your job to squish them into matches.

  • Poke and slosh: drag and poke the belly with your finger to move everything around.
  • Match three or more: line up the same treat and they burst in a shower of color and crumbs.
  • Chain it: string matches together for bigger, brighter combos.

Pick a power

Spend crumbs you earn on powers in the shop, keep them in your inventory, and pick three different ones before a level. Tap a power and it fires instantly.

  • Flavor Pod clears every snack of one kind.
  • Bubble Pod sinks, swells, and pops to shake up the pile.
  • Jelly Bell rolls the belly walls in waves so every snack finds a new spot.
  • Big Gulp gulps 6 to 8 snacks from the middle of the belly.
  • Milkshake Slurp pulls snacks in toward the center.
  • Second Helping starts your next match at x2 and doubles the crumbs you earn.

Watch out for bombs in later levels. They count down with your moves, then blow up nearby snacks. Those snacks still count toward your goals, and bombs never hurt Squishy or take a life.

Play your way

  • Relax on an endless board you can come back to any time.
  • Score to chase your best total.
  • Travel the level path, clear the goals, and unlock new looks with the crumbs you earn.

Dress Squishy up, pick a background, and choose a name from a menu of tasty words. No typing needed, and the name stays on your device.

Fair and free to play

No account and no chat. Optional in-app purchases let you buy gems or a few looks if you want them. Ask a parent or guardian before you buy. Optional ads let you earn extra rewards when you choose to watch one.
