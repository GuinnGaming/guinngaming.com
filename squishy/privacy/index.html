Privacy Policy for Squishy: Full of Snacks

Effective and last updated: November 20, 2026 (adds Google Play Games leaderboards for players 16 and up, and in-app purchases)

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming ("we", "us") handles information when you play Squishy: Full of Snacks (the "Game", previously called Pocket Munch) on Android. The Game is rated for everyone and is made for a mixed audience that includes children. It uses a neutral age screen so that children and players whose age is not known get child-safe ads only, while adults who agree can get personalized ads. Players aged 16 and up can also use optional Google Play Games leaderboards (section 4). The Game offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play (section 5).

1. Information we collect

Saved on your device. The Game saves progress locally: Crumbs (including a running total of Crumbs earned), Gems, owned cosmetics, purchase receipts used to grant and restore items, goals, local best scores, game boards, tutorial progress, comfort settings, the Squishy name you choose, a random local player identifier, and your age-screen answer (see section 2). The Game may also keep local technical logs, which can include game state and rejected saves. GuinnGaming does not receive a copy of this local data. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it from the device, but not records already held by Google or by the purchasing service described in section 5.

Your Squishy name stays on the device. The name you type is used only inside the Game. It is not uploaded. Online leaderboards (section 4) show your Google Play Games display name, never your Squishy name. Parents may want children to pick a made-up name instead of a real one.

Not requested through the Game. There is no GuinnGaming account sign-up, chat or user-generated content shared with other players. The Game does not ask for your email address or phone number and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Service data. Google's advertising service processes information described in section 3, such as IP address, device or other identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. For players 16 and up only, Google Play Games Services processes the information described in section 4. Google Play Billing and the purchasing service process the purchase information described in section 5. No precise location is collected. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address, even though the Game has no Android location permission.

The Game does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication or Firebase Crashlytics, and it does not enable Unity Analytics, Unity Ads or Unity Cloud Diagnostics. This does not mean there is no telemetry: the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends the advertising data described in section 3, Google Play Games Services may send the diagnostics described in section 4, and purchases send the information described in section 5.

Android permissions

These are plain-language explanations of what each permission allows. They do not mean each capability is always in use:

  • INTERNET: lets the Game and its services communicate online, including ads, purchases, and, for players 16 and up, leaderboards.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the Game and its services check whether a network is available.
  • VIBRATE: allows vibration feedback.
  • BILLING (com.android.vending.BILLING): allows purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: let Google's ad libraries finish short background tasks, such as sending queued ad reports. They do not mean the Game runs continuously in the background.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the Android AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. They allow ad identifiers and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs to be used only on the adult path (section 3). For children and players whose age is not known, ad requests are tagged so that the advertising ID is not sent.

An additional app-specific permission, com.guinngaming.squishy.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, restricts access to internal broadcast receivers.

The Game has no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

2. Age screen

The first time you open the Game, it asks for your year of birth on a neutral age screen. It does not encourage any particular answer and has no answer filled in. The Game uses the year only to choose an age bracket, child or adult, and saves only that bracket on your device. The year of birth itself is not stored and is not sent to us or to Google.

A player counts as an adult only if the year shows they are at least 16, counted cautiously (as if their birthday has not happened yet this year). Everyone else is treated as a child. If you tap Not now, nothing is saved, you are treated as a child, and the screen is shown again the next time the Game starts. To change a saved answer, clear the Game's app data in Android settings. Note that this also clears saved progress.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads only. You choose whether to watch one, for example to double a win reward, to get 30 more seconds after a timed loss, or to make snacks arrive more often for 2 minutes in Relaxed mode. The Game does not show banner, interstitial, pop-up or app-open ads, and no ad mediation partners are used. If an ad is not available or does not finish, no reward is given and nothing else changes.

Children and players of unknown age. Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment (Google's age-restricted "child" setting, which covers COPPA child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment). Ads are non-personalized and limited to a maximum content rating of G. With this setting, Google disables interest-based and remarketing ads and the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not send the Android advertising ID. Google's consent form is not shown on this path, and you cannot turn personalized ads on.

Adults. Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of T (Teen). Before ads load, the Game checks privacy choices with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice. In US states with privacy laws that apply, Google's US state privacy message lets you opt out. If you agree, ads may be personalized, based on your choices and only where your device settings allow it (for example, Android lets you delete your advertising ID). If you do not agree, or where personalized ads are not allowed, ads are non-personalized. When your region requires it, an Ad privacy choices entry in the Game's Settings lets you review or change your choices at any time. Ads are not loaded until UMP confirms they may be requested.

Google's ad service processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers (such as app-set or session identifiers, and the advertising ID on the adult path where allowed), ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics. These data support ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and security. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is processed. The Game's ads need an internet connection. When the device is offline, no ads load and the rest of the Game still works.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

4. Google Play Games leaderboards (players 16 and up only)

The Game has three optional online leaderboards in Google Play Games: Best Score (your best Score-mode result), Crumb Jar (the total Crumbs you have earned in the Game; spending Crumbs does not lower it) and Levels Cleared (the highest map level you have cleared).

Only for players 16 and up. Google Play Games Services is started only if the age screen (section 2) shows you are at least 16. For children and for players who tap Not now, the Game does not start Google Play Games at all, does not sign in and sends nothing to it. These players still see their best scores on the device.

What is used. If you are 16 or older, the Game tries to sign you in automatically with your existing Google Play Games profile. Signing in is never required, and the Game works fully without it. If you are signed in, the Game sends your leaderboard scores to Google, linked to your Play Games player ID. Leaderboards show your Play Games display name (gamer name) and avatar with your scores. Who can see them depends on your Play Games profile visibility settings. Google Play Games Services also collects analytics and diagnostics about how the service works. GuinnGaming does not receive your Google account email address. The Game does not use Play Games data for advertising, and it does not read your friends list.

Your choices. You can play without signing in, change your profile visibility in the Play Games app, and delete the Game's Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, from the Play Games app or your Google Account. Google processes Play Games data under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, integrated with Unity IAP. You can buy three Squishy looks for real money — Carnival Cloud, Lotus Glow and Nova Munch — and packs of Gems. Other Squishies and power-ups are unlocked with Gems or Crumbs you earn in the Game, not with a direct real-money purchase. Prices are shown in your local currency on Google Play's purchase screen. Crumbs cannot be bought or exchanged for real money.

Before a real-money purchase starts, the Game shows a confirmation step so you can cancel or continue. Google Play then shows its own purchase screen, and Google's parental controls and purchase verification still apply, including in family groups. Google handles payment. The Game and GuinnGaming never see or store your credit-card or bank details.

Purchase records we keep. When you buy something, Google Play gives the Game a purchase record. It can include the product ID, an order or transaction ID, a purchase token or receipt, the purchase time and its state (for example, completed or refunded). The Game keeps these records only to confirm the purchase, deliver it once, prevent delivering the same purchase twice, and restore eligible items. This information does not include your name, email address or payment-card details. GuinnGaming does not run a separate purchase server that receives this data; the records stay on your device and with the purchasing services that complete the purchase.

Unity IAP, the purchasing service that talks to Google Play, also processes the purchase and transaction information needed to complete a purchase. Unity's disclosure for this service includes purchase history, identifiers used by the service, and diagnostics or other app-performance information. These are service identifiers, not a name you type in the Game. See Unity IAP privacy and consent and Unity IAP's Google Play data disclosure.

Restoring purchases. The three paid Squishy looks can be restored on a new device with the same Google Play account that bought them, using the Game's restore option. Consumable items that have already been delivered, such as Gems, are not restored by Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies. You can request a refund through Google Play, or email us. If a purchase is refunded or reversed, the item it delivered may be removed.

6. How we use information

Local information runs the Game and remembers your progress, settings and age bracket. Purchase information is used only to deliver and restore purchases and to prevent delivering the same purchase twice (section 5). Google processes the information in section 3 for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and security, under the settings described there. For players 16 and up who are signed in, Google Play Games Services uses the information in section 4 to run the leaderboards. We use support emails to answer questions and handle privacy requests.

GuinnGaming does not run a game-account server or receive gameplay data. Leaderboard scores are held by Google Play Games, not by us. We do not sell personal information.

7. Sharing

The Game sends information to Google through AdMob, Google Play Billing and, for signed-in players 16 and up, Google Play Games Services, as described above. It also sends purchase information to Unity through Unity IAP so a purchase can be completed. These providers process information under their service terms and privacy disclosures. Google ad data is listed as shared in our Google Play Data safety section. Unity acts as a service provider for purchasing; the data still leaves your device and goes to Unity.

Support emails are also processed by the email services we use to receive and answer them. Information may be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect rights and safety.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local progress, the Squishy name, the age bracket and local purchase receipts stay on the device until app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. GuinnGaming holds no copy of them. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include the Game's local data in your device backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings. Eligible permanent unlocks can be restored through Google Play after a reinstall; already-delivered Gems are not restored this way.

Google controls how long it keeps advertising records, Play Games leaderboard scores and Play purchase records under its policies. Unity's IAP disclosure describes service logs retained for 30 days and transaction records retained for service and reporting. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete Google's or Unity's records, including Play Games leaderboard scores and Play purchase history. You can delete Play Games data from the Play Games app or your Google Account. You can contact us to ask for access to or deletion of information we hold, and we will help direct requests about provider-held information to the provider's processes.

9. Children and the Google Play Families policy

The Game's target audience includes children, so it follows the Google Play Families policy. Children and players of unknown age get child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which Google lists as a Families Self-Certified Ads SDK. The advertising ID is not sent for these players. Google Play Games Services, including its online leaderboards and automatic sign-in, is never started for children or players of unknown age. The Game has no chat and no GuinnGaming sign-up, and it does not ask for a child's contact information.

Purchases and children. Real-money purchases go through Google Play Billing, so Google Play's parental controls apply. The Game also shows a confirmation step before a purchase starts, so a parent or child can cancel. Google states that verification is always required for every purchase made through Google Play's billing system in an app or game designed for ages 12 and under. A parent in a Google family group can also require approval for purchases, including in-app purchases. The Game keeps the difference between in-game Crumbs or Gems and real-money purchases clear, and it does not use emotionally manipulative tactics to encourage purchases.

For purposes of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GuinnGaming does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what Google's ad service processes under child-directed treatment as described in section 3, and what the purchasing services process to complete a purchase a parent or child chooses to make, as described in section 5. If a parent or guardian contacts us about personal information collected from a child, we will delete it. Contact GuinnGaming87@gmail.com. We will handle information we hold and coordinate deletion of provider-held information through the provider's process.

10. Security

GuinnGaming does not run a game-data backend and never receives or stores payment-card details. Google and Unity process data as described above. The app does not allow unencrypted (cleartext) network traffic, and Google and Unity state that they encrypt data in transit. No electronic system is completely secure.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Adults can change their ad choices at any time through the privacy-options entry described in section 3, where it is offered. Email GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to ask for access to or deletion of your information, or to use other rights you have. We may need to verify your request. We will handle information we hold and help direct requests about provider-held data through the provider's process, subject to legal retention requirements.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or privacy requirements change. We will post the updated version at https://guinngaming.com/squishy/privacy/ and update its date. We will give any additional notice required by law.
