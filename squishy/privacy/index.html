Privacy Policy for Squishy: Full of Snacks

Effective and last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GuinnGaming ("we", "us") handles information when you play Squishy: Full of Snacks (the "Game", previously called Pocket Munch) on Android. The Game is rated for everyone and is made for a mixed audience that includes children. It uses a neutral age screen so that children and players whose age is not known get child-safe ads only, while adults who agree can get personalized ads. The Game has no in-app purchases.

1. Information we collect

Saved on your device. The Game saves progress locally: Crumbs, owned cosmetics, goals, local best scores, game boards, tutorial progress, comfort settings, the Squishy name you choose, a random local player identifier, and your age-screen answer (see section 2). The Game may also keep local technical logs, which can include game state and rejected saves. GuinnGaming does not receive a copy of this local data. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it from the device, but not records already held by Google.

Your Squishy name stays on the device. The name you type is used only inside the Game. It is not uploaded, and there is no online leaderboard. Parents may want children to pick a made-up name instead of a real one.

Not requested through the Game. There is no game account sign-up, chat or user-generated content shared with other players. The Game does not ask for your email address or phone number and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Service data. Google's advertising service processes information described in section 3, such as IP address, device or other identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. No precise location is collected. Approximate location can be inferred from an IP address, even though the Game has no Android location permission.

The Game does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication or Firebase Crashlytics, and it does not enable Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity IAP or Unity Cloud Diagnostics. This does not mean there is no telemetry: the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends the advertising data described in section 3.

Android permissions

These are plain-language explanations of what each permission allows. They do not mean each capability is always in use:

  • INTERNET: lets the Game and its ad service communicate online.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the ad service check whether a network is available.
  • VIBRATE: allows vibration feedback.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: let Google's ad libraries finish short background tasks, such as sending queued ad reports. They do not mean the Game runs continuously in the background.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the Android AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. They allow ad identifiers and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs to be used only on the adult path (section 3). For children and players whose age is not known, ad requests are tagged so that the advertising ID is not sent.

An additional app-specific permission, com.guinngaming.squishy.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, restricts access to internal broadcast receivers.

The Game has no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or Google Play Billing permissions.

2. Age screen

The first time you open the Game, it asks for your year of birth on a neutral age screen. It does not encourage any particular answer and has no answer filled in. The Game uses the year only to choose an age bracket, child or adult, and saves only that bracket on your device. The year of birth itself is not stored and is not sent to us or to Google.

A player counts as an adult only if the year shows they are at least 16, counted cautiously (as if their birthday has not happened yet this year). Everyone else is treated as a child. If you tap Not now, nothing is saved, you are treated as a child, and the screen is shown again the next time the Game starts. To change a saved answer, clear the Game's app data in Android settings. Note that this also clears saved progress.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads only. You choose whether to watch one, for example to double a win reward, to get 30 more seconds after a timed loss, or to make snacks arrive more often for 2 minutes in Relaxed mode. The Game does not show banner, interstitial, pop-up or app-open ads, and no ad mediation partners are used. If an ad is not available or does not finish, no reward is given and nothing else changes.

Children and players of unknown age. Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment (Google's age-restricted "child" setting, which covers COPPA child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment). Ads are non-personalized and limited to a maximum content rating of G. With this setting, Google disables interest-based and remarketing ads and the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not send the Android advertising ID. Google's consent form is not shown on this path, and you cannot turn personalized ads on.

Adults. Ads are limited to a maximum content rating of T (Teen). Before ads load, the Game checks privacy choices with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice. In US states with privacy laws that apply, Google's US state privacy message lets you opt out. If you agree, ads may be personalized, based on your choices and only where your device settings allow it (for example, Android lets you delete your advertising ID). If you do not agree, or where personalized ads are not allowed, ads are non-personalized. When your region requires it, an Ad privacy choices entry in the Game's Settings lets you review or change your choices at any time. Ads are not loaded until UMP confirms they may be requested.

Google's ad service processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers (such as app-set or session identifiers, and the advertising ID on the adult path where allowed), ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics. These data support ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and security. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data is processed. The Game's ads need an internet connection. When the device is offline, no ads load and the rest of the Game still works.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

4. In-app purchases

The Game does not offer in-app purchases. Crumbs and cosmetics are earned through play and have no real-money value. No payment is processed, and the Game does not receive credit-card or bank details.

5. How we use information

Local information runs the Game and remembers your progress, settings and age bracket. Google processes the information in section 3 for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and security, under the settings described there. We use support emails to answer questions and handle privacy requests.

GuinnGaming does not run a game-account server or receive gameplay data. We do not sell personal information.

6. Sharing

The Game sends information to Google through AdMob, as described above. Google processes it under its service terms and privacy disclosures. Google ad data is listed as shared in our Google Play Data safety section.

Support emails are also processed by the email services we use to receive and answer them. Information may be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect rights and safety.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local progress, the Squishy name and the age bracket stay on the device until app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. GuinnGaming holds no copy of them. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include the Game's local data in your device backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings.

Google controls how long it keeps advertising records under its policies. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete Google's records. You can contact us to ask for access to or deletion of information we hold, and we will help direct requests about Google-held information to Google's processes.

8. Children and the Google Play Families policy

The Game's target audience includes children, so it follows the Google Play Families policy. Children and players of unknown age get child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which Google lists as a Families Self-Certified Ads SDK. The advertising ID is not sent for these players. The Game has no chat, no sign-up, no online leaderboard and no purchases, and it does not ask for a child's contact information.

For purposes of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GuinnGaming does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what Google's ad service processes under child-directed treatment as described in section 3. If a parent or guardian contacts us about personal information collected from a child, we will delete it. Contact GuinnGaming87@gmail.com. We will handle information we hold and coordinate deletion of provider-held information through the provider's process.

9. Security

GuinnGaming does not run a game-data backend and does not store payment details. Google processes data as described above. The app does not allow unencrypted (cleartext) network traffic, and Google states that it encrypts data in transit. No electronic system is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Adults can change their ad choices at any time through the privacy-options entry described in section 3, where it is offered. Email GuinnGaming87@gmail.com to ask for access to or deletion of your information, or to use other rights you have. We may need to verify your request. We will handle information we hold and help direct requests about provider-held data through the provider's process, subject to legal retention requirements.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or privacy requirements change. We will post the updated version at https://guinngaming.com/squishy/privacy/ and update its date. We will give any additional notice required by law.
